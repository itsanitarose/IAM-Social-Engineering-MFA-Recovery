
# 05. Privileged Account Recovery

## 1. How Can We Recover MFA Without Putting a Privileged Account at Risk?

> What happens when a finance approver or administrator loses access to their MFA device? They need to regain access, but the recovery process must not create an opportunity for account takeover.

I've designed a 5 step recovery process built around **identity verification, separation of duties and evidence based validation**.

The person verifying identity should not approve recovery, and the person approving recovery should not execute it. Suspicious activity must also be investigated before recovery proceeds.

## 2. My Proposed Five-Step Recovery Process

| Step | Owner | Recovery Actions | Core Security Principle | Risk Reduced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Record & Triage** | Help Desk / IAM | Create a ticket with caller details, affected account and reason for recovery. Compare the calling number against HR records. IAM checks account privileges, registered MFA methods and recent sign-in activity. | **Traceability** | Rushed or undocumented recovery requests. |
| **2. Verify Identity** | Help Desk / Identity Verification Team | Verify through a previously established contact channel or approved identity-proofing method. Do not rely on employee ID, DOB or manager's name. Escalate refused verification or suspicious contact changes. | **Independent Verification** | Impersonation using stolen personal information. |
| **3. Review & Authorise** | IAM / Independent Security Approver | Review verification evidence, account privileges, risk indicators and recent MFA changes. Require separate approval for finance approvers and administrators. The approver cannot execute the reset. | **Separation of Duties** | Unauthorised recovery and single-person control. |
| **4. Restore MFA Securely** | Authorised IAM Administrator | Use an approved recovery mechanism, such as a short-lived, preferably single-use Entra Temporary Access Pass (TAP). Deliver it securely, register the replacement authenticator and remove the lost method. Coordinate session revocation or device containment if compromise is suspected. | **Controlled, Time-Limited Recovery** | Unauthorised authenticator registration and recovery credential exposure. |
| **5. Validate & Close** | IAM / Security | Confirm successful registration and authentication. Review sign-in and audit logs for unexpected activity. Verify required containment actions, notify the employee through a trusted channel and document the outcome. | **Evidence-Based Validation** | Suspicious account changes being overlooked during recovery. |

### Important Technical Considerations

- **Failed verification:** Pause recovery and escalate for security review. Account blocking is a separate risk-based decision.
- **Temporary Access Pass:** Microsoft documents a default lifetime of one hour. TAP creation requires appropriate Entra administrative permissions and must comply with Conditional Access policies.
- **Session revocation:** Revoking sessions may not immediately terminate all existing access tokens or application sessions.
- **Proposed policy:** Independent verification, approval and execution are separate responsibilities for sensitive account recovery at Northwind Health.

## 3. Additional Control — Independent Financial Transaction Approval

Because Dana can approve payments and access the banking portal, I would introduce **independent approval for high-risk financial transactions**.

A second authorised individual would need to approve qualifying payments using their own authenticated account. The same person could not complete every required approval stage.

**What this limits:** If an attacker compromises Dana's account, the compromised account alone may not be sufficient to complete a high-risk payment.

For administrative accounts, just-in-time access through Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management (PIM) is another relevant safeguard, although it does not automatically control banking application permissions.

## 4. My Takeaway

**A secure MFA recovery process should verify identity, require independent authorisation, restrict who can perform the reset, and validate the outcome.**

Successful sign-in alone does not mean recovery was secure.
