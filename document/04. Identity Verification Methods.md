
# 04. Identity Verification Methods

## 1. How Do We Know the Caller Is Really Dana?

The caller knew Dana's employee ID, manager's name and date of birth. But does knowing these details actually prove her identity?

**I assessed five verification methods to understand which provide stronger identity assurance and which could leave the MFA recovery process vulnerable to social engineering.**

## 2. Strong vs Weak Verification Methods

| Verification Method | Rating |  Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Employee ID | **Weak** | An employee ID identifies someone in company records, but it isn't a secret. Someone else could obtain it. |
| Date of birth | **Weak** | This information can be found or exposed. Knowing it doesn't prove the caller is Dana. |
| Callback to the number on file | **Strong (conditional)** | The agent contacts a previously verified number rather than trusting the incoming call. However, the number could still be compromised. |
| Manager approval through a known channel | **Strong (supporting)** | Independently contacting the manager can confirm the business need and authorisation, but doesn't independently prove the caller's identity. |
| Live video call showing photo ID | **Weak on its own** | A photo ID or video can be manipulated. Stronger assurance would require a properly controlled identity-proofing process. |

*Strong does not mean sufficient on its own, particularly for an account with sensitive financial access.*
